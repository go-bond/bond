Phase 1 crypto11 existing-path compatibility probe
Captured 2026-08-06 with go1.26.5 linux/amd64.

This is retained research evidence, not a final graph requirement. `go mod
why -m github.com/ThalesGroup/crypto11` reports that the main module does not
need this module, and final `go mod tidy` leaves no crypto11, pkcs11, or pool
requirement or checksum in Bond's go.mod/go.sum.

If a future Bond package needs the old crypto11 module path, v1.6.7 is the
latest release verified compatible with that path:

$ go get github.com/ThalesGroup/crypto11@v1.6.7

go: upgraded github.com/miekg/pkcs11 v1.1.1 => v1.1.2
